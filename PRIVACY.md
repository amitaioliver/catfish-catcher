# Privacy policy

Catfish Catcher for LinkedIn collects no data and sends nothing anywhere.

**What it reads.** LinkedIn pages you open, and the profiles of flagged post authors outside your network. Reading happens in your browser only.

**What it stores, in your browser only:**
- your settings,
- profile check results, for 7 days,
- short fingerprints of post text, for 30 days, to spot copied posts.

**What it sends.** Nothing. There are no servers, analytics or ads. The two optional links (LinkedIn search and Google Lens) open in a new tab only when you click them.

**Removing data.** Uninstalling the extension deletes everything it stored.
